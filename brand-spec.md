# Superblock Analytics Studio — Brand and Interface Specification

## Reference audit

The live Superblock product at `https://dev.superblock.chat/home` uses an information-dense white canvas, a narrow persistent sidebar, compact hairline controls, black typography, and sparing lime/green status signals. Navigation is grouped into platform and app sections, the header is intentionally quiet, and cards rely on white space and thin neutral boundaries rather than heavy shadows. The official public site positions Superblock as an AI-powered customer communication operating system spanning WhatsApp, email, SMS, RCS, voice, shared inboxes, broadcasts, automation, and customer engagement.

## Design read

- Artifact: responsive internal customer-operations and analytics dashboard
- Audience: Superblock executives, operations, finance, sales, support, and customer-success teams
- Visual language: precision operations console with editorial hierarchy
- Mode: greenfield extension of the live Superblock product language
- Visual variance: 5/10
- Motion intensity: 3/10
- Information density: 9/10
- Asset dependence: 3/10
- Brand fidelity: 8/10

## Proposed system

- Anchor: Superblock live app, interpreted through restrained Linear/Stripe-like operational clarity
- Palette: warm white canvas, ink black typography, cool-gray surfaces, acid-lime primary signal, emerald success, amber warning, coral risk, indigo analytic comparison
- Typography: Google Sans for UI and display, with Google Sans Code or a native monospace fallback for identifiers and tabular micro-labels
- Spacing: 4 px base grid with dense 12/16/20/24 px component rhythms
- Radius: compact 8–12 px controls and cards; pills only for statuses and segmented filters
- Shadows: mostly borders; one low, diffuse elevation for floating menus, dialogs, and drawers
- Motion: 120–220 ms; immediate keyboard interactions, restrained hover transitions, no decorative motion
- Data visualization: low-gridline Recharts, semantic channel colors, compact legends, high data-ink ratio

Google Sans is the defining typographic adjustment requested by the user and should be applied throughout navigation, controls, headings, tables, and chart labels.
