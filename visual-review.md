# Visual Review Findings

Desktop views at 1440 × 900 were reviewed for Analytics Studio, Customers, Customer Detail, Products & Plans, Settings, and Login. The layout is consistent with the approved Superblock direction: crisp white surfaces, compact information density, acid-lime primary actions, Google Sans typography, restrained borders, and clear semantic status colors. Charts render correctly without clipping, table columns align, customer health visuals remain legible, and the split login page has appropriate contrast.

Tablet views at 768 × 900 were reviewed for Analytics Studio and Customer Detail. The desktop sidebar correctly collapses into a mobile navigation trigger; KPI grids reduce to two columns; toolbars wrap without overflow; the customer action row reflows; tabs remain horizontally scrollable; and charts retain readable axis labels. No visible overflow, contrast, spacing, or truncation defects were found in the reviewed states.
